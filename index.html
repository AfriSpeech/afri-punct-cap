Pan-African Multilingual Punctuation & Capitalization Engine

Fine-tuned on 3.3 Million multi-sentence passages across 751 African languages (Bantu, Kwa, Chadic, Ethiopic, Nilotic) to restore commas, sentence-ending periods, and capitalization in unpunctuated ASR transcripts and text. Compensates for acoustic breath pauses typically omitted when writing African languages.

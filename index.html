Pan-African Punctuation & Capitalization Demo

Trained on 3.3 Million multi-sentence passages across 751 African languages and dialects. The model operates with zero language tags: input raw text in any African language and it restores commas, sentence-ending periods, and proper capitalization based on generalized syntactic and prosodic boundary patterns.

Serverless Modal INT8 (Fast CPU Engine)
Test sentences are 100% held-out from the initial audio-text corpus (never seen during training).
INT8 runs in ~15–25ms on CPU with 50% smaller footprint.
